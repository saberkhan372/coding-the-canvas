One period = one concept

Each of the 22 concepts is designed for a single class period of 30–50 minutes: 10 min demo, 20 min practice + remix, 10 min checkpoint. Adjust freely — some teachers split a concept across two shorter sessions.

Where to cut

If time is short, cut in this order: §7 Algorithms last (CS A teachers should keep it), §6 Impact second (can be done as reading + discussion without canvas time), §5 Data sections 14–16 first (least essential of the data block).

Where not to cut

§4 Make Systems is the spine. Loops, functions, arrays, and objects are the heart of every AP exam. Give this section the most time. If you only teach one section in depth, make it §4.
