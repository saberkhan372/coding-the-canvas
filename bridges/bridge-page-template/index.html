Reusable Bridge Page Template

A structured pattern for turning a fuzzy concept into intuition, vocabulary, a short demo, and a next step.

Use this for every concept bridge.

A bridge is not a full lesson and not a starter sketch. It is the short experiential explanation you open when an idea is slippery.

  • Start from the misconception students are likely carrying.
  • Give one visible model and one prediction question.
  • End by pointing to a tool, sketch, or concept page.

Page sections

Common misconception

Name the wrong-but-reasonable idea students may bring. Example: “Arrays are just a shorter way to name variables.”

Discussion question

Ask a question that makes the model visible. Example: “If you add one item to the array, what changes in the loop code?”

Vocabulary

List exact terms and functions: array, index, length, push(), for loop, off-by-one.

3-minute demo

Script the facilitation: open the visual, make one change, ask for a prediction, reveal the common mistake.

Paired tool + sketch

Give the natural next action: one tool for guided play and one starter sketch for remixing.
