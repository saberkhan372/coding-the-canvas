About Coding the Canvas

A creative coding for all curriculum shaped by CC Fest: visual first, beginner-safe, classroom-ready, and open to remix.

community care

Beginners protected. Questions count as participation. Unfinished work is welcome.

Come in. You can learn here. You can ask questions. You can make something. You do not have to be an expert to begin — or to belong.

Code should do something students can see.

The curriculum treats the canvas as a shared thinking space. Every abstraction earns its place by changing a drawing, animation, image, or interaction.

  • Visual first, vocabulary second.
  • Creative work tied to CS fundamentals.
  • One concept sequence, several language routes.

design promise

VisibleEvery abstraction should change something students can see.
DoableEach page should offer a clear first move, not just an explanation.
RemixableExamples should invite students and teachers to change them.
CarefulCreative agency, consent, and beginner safety are part of the design.

Audience

Middle school, high school, workshops, and AP-adjacent classrooms can all use the same core path with different pacing.

Approach

Students move from concrete canvas actions to loops, arrays, objects, data, impact, recursion, and Java-ready structures.

Tone

Lessons are meant to feel exploratory, precise, and useful without turning creativity into decoration.
