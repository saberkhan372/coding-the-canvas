CC Fest

Creative Coding Fest — a free, community-driven festival that treats code as a language for making meaning, telling stories, and engaging with the world. The spirit behind Coding the Canvas.

A rainy Saturday at NYU ITP, October 2016.

Forty people showed up. Dan Shiffman gave a keynote on tool neutrality. Teachers built classroom projects while students made creative portfolios. No one left empty-handed — everyone left with something visible on a canvas. That was the idea: code should produce something you can point to, explain, and keep making.

CC Fest has been running that idea forward ever since — through in-person events in New York, Los Angeles, and San Francisco; through a virtual pivot in 2020 that made it global; through Teacher Training Camps that started in 2024. Ten years, and still 100% free.

  • Code as creative and cultural expression, not just a technical skill.
  • Hands-on coding with immediate visual results.
  • No prerequisites. No judgment. Just curiosity.
  • Peer teaching, where students become mentors.

Ten years of milestones

October 2016 — NYU ITP, New York City

40 people on a rainy Saturday. Keynote by Dan Shiffman on tool neutrality. Workshops on p5.js basics, animation, and geometric aesthetics. The idea: code should produce something you can point to and keep making.

2019 — Expanded to LA and San Francisco

CC Fest reaches UCLA Design|Media Arts (led by Casey Reas and Lauren Lee McCarthy) and SF Friends School. Creative coding becomes a West Coast conversation too.

July 2020 — First virtual CC Fest

Pandemic pivot. The event goes online and immediately becomes global — participants from 20+ countries join for the first time. Raised funds for TRANSIT ART youth program.

January 2023 — Return to in-person, Dalton School NYC

180 registrants from 30 institutions. Co-organized by Dalton students and CS faculty. Keynotes by Dan Shiffman and Christy Crawford. The in-person energy is back.

Fall 2024 — First Teacher Training Camp

A new program line begins: a free, multi-week virtual professional development camp for educators. 10 sessions over 3 months. Designed and led by practicing creative-coding educators.

Spring 2026 — 10th year, 12 countries

The Spring 2026 Teacher Camp spans 12 countries and 1,700+ students reachable through participants. The public tools library hits 66 interactive tools and 44 starter sketches.

CC Fest Teacher Training Camp

Professional development built by educators, for educators.

Launched in Fall 2024, the Teacher Camp is a free, five-week virtual program that helps educators bring creative coding into their classrooms with confidence and purpose. Each session combines hands-on p5.js coding, guest artists and educators, and collaborative discussion about classroom implementation.

Graduates leave with a portfolio of classroom-ready projects, adaptable lesson plans, and membership in a growing global network of educators committed to reimagining CS education as a space for creativity, inclusion, and joy.

  • 5 sessions · 2 hours each · 100% free
  • Foundation, Exploration, and Classroom Adaptation tracks
  • Guest speakers from the creative coding community
  • Async recordings posted after every session
